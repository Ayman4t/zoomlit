Features

Everything a polished demo needs.

Smart zoom, focus tools and export presets, built into one browser tab.

Zoom that follows your cursor.

Clicks and pauses trigger smooth zooms, so viewers always see what you are doing. Pick a zoom style, add a cursor glow, motion trail and click ripples.

Spotlight and neon pen.

Dim everything except the part that matters, then draw arrows, circles and highlights that fade away on their own. Press L for spotlight and P for the pen.

Hide what you did not mean to show.

Paint the censor brush over emails, tokens and names before you export. Covered areas stay covered for the whole clip.

About us

Making good demos should not need a studio.

Zoomlit Studio is a free browser-based video editor made for creators made by AymanStack. We built it for people who record their screen to teach, sell, support or show off their work, and want the result to feel directed rather than captured.

Everything happens in a browser tab: no installs, no accounts, no complicated timelines. You bring the recording, Zoomlit handles the camera moves and polish.
